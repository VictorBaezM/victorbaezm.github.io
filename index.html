About me

I'm a Puerto Rican software developer who is passionate about creating useful, user-centered software solutions. I have a solid foundation in producing dependable and maintainable code while resolving challenging technical problems thanks to my expertise in software development, testing, and debugging. I have worked with a range of programming languages, frameworks, and technologies throughout my projects, and I am adept at swiftly adjusting to new tools and development environments. While constantly honing my technical skill set, I like developing apps that blend performance, utility, and clean design. My work approach is centered on problem-solving and teamwork. I enjoy collaborating directly with people, sharing ideas, and participating in initiatives that have a significant influence. With a bachelor's degree in software engineering and three years of testing and debugging-focused work experience at L3Harris, I offer both technical expertise and a meticulous approach to every project I work on.
